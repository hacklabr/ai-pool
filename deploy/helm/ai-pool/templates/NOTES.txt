  ⚠️  CRITICAL: in production (DEV_MODE=false), the pod will NOT start if the
  Secret "{{ include "ai-pool.appSecretName" . }}" is missing. Create it first:

     KEK=$(openssl rand -base64 32)
     PEPPER=$(openssl rand -base64 48)
     kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} create secret generic {{ include "ai-pool.appSecretName" . }} \
       --from-literal=kek="$KEK" \
       --from-literal=token_pepper="$PEPPER"
     # SALVAR KEK E PEPPER EM COFRE OFFLINE (perdeu = perdeu todas as chaves)
